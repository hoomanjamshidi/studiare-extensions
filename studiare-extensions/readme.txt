Extra features for the Studiare LMS theme: a customizable mobile bottom navigation, Elementor page templates for courses, products, headers, footers and the blog, ready-made home, about us and contact us pages, a floating support button, a "Save as PDF" button and podcast player for blog posts, audio products that buyers listen to on the site, and fixes for known theme problems.

== Description ==

= Mobile bottom navigation =

* Five styles: Classic, Floating glass, Center button (notch), Bubble and Expanding pill — each with a live preview.
* Any number of buttons (up to 8); the layout adapts so it always looks balanced.
* Button types: home, custom link, search sheet, cart (live WooCommerce count, opens the Studiare mini cart), account (avatar and guest label), menu, content sheet (Elementor/shortcode), back to top, dark mode, and "click an element".
* Six bundled SVG icon packs (Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor, Phosphor Duotone, Heroicons, Bootstrap Icons) plus Studiare's Font Awesome, custom images or custom SVG per button.
* Colours, fonts and dark mode follow the Studiare theme by default; every colour, size and font can be overridden.
* Cache friendly (no server-side device detection), accessible (keyboard, screen readers, reduced motion) and RTL-first.

= Page templates (Elementor) =

* 36 ready-made designs, installed as normal Elementor templates: 3 course pages, 3 product pages, 7 headers, 8 footers, 3 home pages, 3 about us pages, 3 contact us pages, 3 blog post lists and 3 blog posts.
* Pages: pick a home, about us or contact us design and create a real WordPress page from it in one click (a home design can become the site's home page), then edit it like any Elementor page. Products, categories, posts and Studiare teachers fill in from the site.
* Blog: one design for post lists (the blog page, categories, tags, authors, dates and blog searches, with page numbers) and one for single posts, with a table of contents, share buttons, author box, related posts and Studiare's own comments.
* Use Studiare's own layout or a template — for all courses/products, per product category (with or without subcategories), or per product from its edit screen.
* Separate header and footer for desktop and for phones/tablets (template, the theme's own, or nothing). Both are printed and switched with CSS, so page caching keeps working.
* 68 Elementor widgets in a "Studiare+" category: product title, price, add to cart (variations, quantity, "already enrolled", "in cart"), gallery with the course intro video, course facts, curriculum from Studiare's lessons, teacher, tabs, reviews, related products, mobile buy bar, logo, menu with mobile drawer, search, cart, login, dark mode switch, copyright (Solar Hijri year), e-Namad badges; for home pages: a fast slider in six designs, hero slides, promo card, product grid (seven card designs, category buttons), product spotlight with a sale countdown, category grid, post grid (blog, magazine, podcasts), features, countdown, offer price, testimonials, teachers & team, events, FAQ (with FAQ structured data), key numbers, picture/video (YouTube, Aparat, Vimeo), logo strip, pricing plans and a newsletter form with a built-in list (CSV download, WordPress privacy tools); for about and contact pages: contact form, map, contact details and timeline; and for the blog: page title, breadcrumb, categories, post title, post details, featured image, post content, table of contents, share buttons, tags, author box, previous/next post, comments and a reading progress bar.
* Persian-first: RTL layouts, Persian digits and separators, widgets inherit the site's Persian font instead of Elementor's default fonts.
* Brand colours in one place (with Studiare dark mode); container "brand surface" and "sticky" options in Elementor.
* Live preview of every design on desktop, tablet and phone before saving.
* Needs Elementor (free) 3.16 or newer with Flexbox Containers; tested with Elementor 3.35 and 4.3 and WooCommerce 11.

= Floating support button =

* One corner button for Telegram, WhatsApp, Bale, Eitaa, Instagram, phone, email and any support page. Type a username, a number (Persian digits and 09… mobiles work) or a link; the admin shows the exact link each channel opens.
* Two designs: a card with a heading and one row per channel, or round brand-coloured bubbles. A single channel turns the button into a direct link with that app's logo.
* WhatsApp can open with a ready-made first message; an optional greeting appears once per visit after a delay you choose.
* Rises automatically above the Studiare+ bottom navigation, sticky buy bars and the theme's "back to top" button, and follows Studiare's colours and dark mode.
* Works without JavaScript (a native disclosure), is cache friendly, and is keyboard and screen reader accessible.

= Cart & checkout =

* New designs for WooCommerce's cart and checkout pages (the classic pages Studiare uses): purchase steps (cart, details & payment, access to the courses or order tracking), items as cards, a sticky order summary beside the form (a folding bar on phones), payment methods as cards and a total bar at the bottom of phones. The cart updates a moment after a quantity changes.
* A coupon box in the summary that shows its answer, right or wrong, under the box instead of a bar at the top; removing a coupon answers there too. WooCommerce's notices are restyled, and cart and checkout messages are in Persian even without WooCommerce's Persian language pack.
* A mini cart to match (the cart that opens from the header and the bottom navigation): items as cards with "Free" for free courses, the total and what sale prices and coupons save, the checkout button first and always in view while the list scrolls, and an inviting empty state. Works in dark mode; the theme's design can be kept.
* A thank-you page to match: an animated tick (with a little confetti after a successful payment), the buyer's name, buttons to their courses or order, the order number, date, total and payment method, a "Ready to start" card for each purchase with its own buttons (start the course, download each file, listen to an audio product in "My audio") and a "What happens next" timeline. Bank transfer, cash on delivery and failed payments each get their own wording and colours (a failed payment offers to pay again). The gateways' details, downloads and addresses are restyled as cards.
* Billing field settings: switch each field on or off, rename it, make it required or optional, choose half or full width and drag fields into order. A cart of only courses and downloads asks only for the fields marked for it (by default name, mobile and email). Add your own fields (text, number, phone, email, list, checkbox, national code with check-digit validation); their answers are saved with the order and shown in the order screen, emails and order details. Mobile numbers typed with Persian digits, +98 or 0098 are accepted and saved as 09….
* Works with Persian WooCommerce and Persian WooCommerce Shipping (Iranian provinces, cities and districts, Pishtaz, courier), shipping to another address, free shipping and every payment gateway. Each page can keep the theme's design while the field settings still apply. Off until switched on.

= Article tools =

* "Save as PDF" on every blog post: a button above the text, a box after it, or both. It opens the browser's print window, where "Save as PDF" is one of the printers, so Persian text, fonts and pictures come out exactly as on the site. Only the article is printed (title, author, date, featured picture, text, a link back to the post and page numbers), also with the browser's own Print command and in dark mode. Authors can hide the button on a single post.
* Podcast player at the top of posts that have an audio version: choose the file from the media library (or paste a link) in the post editor's "Studiare+ article tools" box, with an episode title, narrator, cover and duration. Waveform timeline, 15 seconds back / 30 forward, playback speed, download, "continue where you stopped" and the phone's lock screen controls. Two looks (soft, following dark mode, or an always dark studio). Nothing is downloaded until play is tapped, and without JavaScript the browser's own player shows.

= Audio products =

* Sell audio (audio books, courses, podcasts) as WooCommerce products: add the tracks in the product editor's "Studiare+ audio" box, and buyers listen on the product page and in a "My audio" tab of their account, not only by downloading.
* A free preview for every track: its first seconds (MP3; the site sends only that part of the file), a separate short preview file, the whole track free, or none. When a preview ends the player offers the buy button.
* Files uploaded from the box go to WooCommerce's protected folder and are sent by the site only to visitors allowed to hear them, with seeking; their address never appears on the page. Links to another file host work too.
* Full access only for customers who paid for the product (not subscriptions, roles or being logged in). Downloads for buyers can be switched off per product.
* The audio of a course's Studiare lessons can join the player, with the access set in Studiare (open, logged-in users, buyers, release dates).
* Playlist player with 15 seconds back / 30 forward, previous/next, playback speed, "continue where you stopped", lock screen controls, dark mode and two looks.

= Theme fixes =

* Repairs known problems of the Studiare theme without editing its files, so the fixes survive theme updates. Every fix has its own switch, and the admin shows whether the theme feature it repairs is in use on the site.
* Persian digits in the mobile login: the OTP forms accept numbers typed with a Persian keyboard, pasted with spaces, or written with +98, 0098 or without the first zero.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `studiare-extensions` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate the plugin.
3. Open **Studiare+ → Mobile bottom navigation**, **Studiare+ → Page templates**, **Studiare+ → Floating support button**, **Studiare+ → Cart & checkout** or **Studiare+ → Theme fixes** in the admin menu.

* New: Cart & checkout module (Studiare+ → Cart & checkout). Compatible with Persian WooCommerce and Persian WooCommerce Shipping.

The plugin's listeners now react at once and Rocket no longer replays taps they handled.
